An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **The One-Line Laugh** — twelve hours, one funny line each, judged pairwise by a house judge.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes a single funny sentence to `/output/entry.txt`: a bank app congratulating its owner for saving money, right after watching them spend $40 on a candle that smells like "rain". Deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output.
